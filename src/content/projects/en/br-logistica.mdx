## The problem

**BR Logística** is small on the admin side and large in fleet. I did the analysis with the owner: there was no inventory system, only Excel. Stock did not drop on dispatch, and product expired in the warehouse.

I recommended lot-and-expiry control, outbound picking with **automatic FEFO** (so it would not depend on the operator), plus customers, returns, and adjustments for inbound/outbound traceability.

## What I built

An internal WMS in production, with users and roles: lot-level inventory (stock is queried, not edited by hand), receiving, picking, suppliers, customers, returns, and adjustments. Stock only moves through documents.

## Technical decisions

Same approach as Automotive: a **modular monolith** to keep cost down — one deploy, inventory and documents as separate domains. **React + TypeScript** SPA (Cloudflare, TanStack Query) and **Java + Spring Boot** API (Railway) on **PostgreSQL**, JWT, and Flyway.

## The hard problem

Confirming a pick has to allocate the right lot by FEFO. Returns go back **to the original lot**, without breaking that ledger. The flow is `DRAFT → CONFIRMED`: stock does not change until the document is confirmed.

## The outcome

Inbound/outbound traceability, inventory that moves on its own, less loss to expiry, and faster cycle counts and delivery prep.
