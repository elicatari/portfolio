Independent paid work for **Happy Pet**, built in parallel with other roles (2025–2026).

## The problem

**Happy Pet** kept the schedule on paper and coordinated over WhatsApp. Clients did not know if a transfer was available until they asked and waited for a reply.

## What I built

A public real-time booking calendar: the client signs up, adds their pet, picks a day and time, and **sees transfer availability** without going through staff.

## Technical decisions

Small business: a **modular monolith**, one deploy. **React + TypeScript** SPA (Cloudflare) and **Java + Spring Boot** API (Railway) on **PostgreSQL**.

## The hard problem

Only **4 transfers per day**. If two clients request the last one at the same time, one has to lose. The cap — and the time slot — is reserved in the database, not with an `if` in the API.

## The outcome

Self-service: the client sees real availability and books without a WhatsApp back-and-forth.
